Make it a record book

Same form, same ten teams, every July. Keep four running numbers per manager — spots off, exact hits, career homer index, and championships correctly called — and inside three seasons you'll know whether Nick is actually good at this or just had one summer. Two rules worth adopting now: one ballot per person, and no aliases unless the alias is announced at the draft.
